Open a .pub file on iPhone or Android

A .pub file is a Microsoft Publisher document, and there is no Publisher app for phones. If someone emailed you a .pub — a flyer, a newsletter, an invitation — your phone won't open it on its own.

The fix works on any phone and needs no app: open the file in your mobile browser, which converts it to a PDF you can read. Your file stays on your phone — nothing is uploaded.

Open your .pub on your phone now — free for one file, nothing leaves your device. Open the converter

On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open PubRescue in Safari.
  2. Tap the page, then Choose File → pick the .pub from Files, iCloud Drive, or save it from the email first.
  3. Tap Download PDF. It opens in the iPhone viewer and can be saved or shared.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Open PubRescue in Chrome.
  2. Tap to choose the .pub file from Files or Google Drive.
  3. Tap Download PDF — it opens in your PDF viewer and saves to the phone.

The conversion runs inside the browser on your phone, so the file is never sent to a server.

On a computer instead?

It works the same in any desktop browser. See open a .pub without Publisher, or the guides for Mac and Chromebook.
