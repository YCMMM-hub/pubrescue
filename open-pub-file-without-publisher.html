How to open a .pub file without Publisher

A .pub file is a Microsoft Publisher document. Since Microsoft retired Publisher from Microsoft 365 in October 2026, many people are stuck with .pub files they can no longer open — Word, Google Docs and Mac Preview all refuse them.

The simplest and safest fix is to convert the file to PDF right in your browser. Nothing to install, and your file is never uploaded to a server.

Open your .pub file now — free for one file at a time, your file never leaves your device. Open the converter

Step by step

  1. Open PubRescue in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
  2. Drag your .pub file onto the page, or click to pick it from your device.
  3. After about a second, click Download PDF. It opens in your usual viewer and you can save it anywhere.

The conversion runs inside your browser: the file stays on your device. That is what sets PubRescue apart from the usual online converters.

What about the alternatives?

  • Free online converters: they work, but they upload your file to their servers. Not ideal for a personal letter, a newsletter or a company document.
  • LibreOffice: free, but it's a full office suite to install, and its .pub support is hit or miss.
  • A Windows PC that still has Publisher: if you can borrow one, File › Save As › PDF works — but Publisher is gone from Microsoft 365 and never existed on Mac.
